# Rasta design system

## Direction
A student in Lahore researching universities at a bright kitchen table, using a laptop or a shared phone. The workspace should reduce anxiety through legible light surfaces, clear next steps and a reassuring rose identity.

## Palette
Use the OKLCH tokens in src/styles.css. Pure white primary surfaces, near-neutral grey canvas, deep rose primary oklch(.51 .16 350), quiet rose selected surfaces, dark plum ink. Green indicates completed or minimum eligibility; amber indicates a requirement to check. Campus monograms are typographic identifiers rather than official logos.

## Typography
Self-hosted DM Sans, weights 400/500/600/700. Product headings 30–36px, modest line lengths, clear field labels. Small metadata stays secondary.

## Layout
Desktop side navigation with seven views; mobile navigation drawer. Dashboard: ordered journey strip, immediate next action, test scenario preview, saved universities, essay and application actions. Forms use two columns on wide screens and one on phones. Dialogs are native modal dialogs.

## Interactions
150–200ms state transitions, visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion override. Profile saves explicitly; essays and applications save on edit. Sample profile is labelled until replaced. Data can be exported, imported and cleared with confirmation.
