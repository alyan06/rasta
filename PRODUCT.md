# Rasta

## Register
product

## Users
Pakistani students studying Matric/FSc or O/A Levels, often researching admissions on a phone, with limited access to counselling. They apply in Pakistan and the United States.

## Product Purpose
A free workspace to understand university requirements, build an authentic student profile, model test scores, prepare essays and manage applications.

## Brand Personality
Welcoming, clear, encouraging. A patient older student who knows the process.

## Anti-references
GPA-only onboarding, unsupported admission probabilities, aggressive paid upgrades, and dashboards that overwhelm a first-time applicant.

## Design Principles
- Start with the student's own qualification system.
- Give a concrete next step on each screen.
- Keep official requirements distinct from planning assumptions.
- Support student authorship and preserve their voice.
- Keep the first version free and useful without API keys.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable light surfaces for daytime phone and laptop use, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, responsive navigation, reduced motion, and plain English with Pakistani terminology. Urdu interface is a future enhancement.
